# academic-calm-space
A simple web-based Study Tracker that helps students log study sessions, monitor time spent on subjects, and build consistent study habits. It promotes better time management and productivity during exam preparation. 


Study Tracker – Academic Calm Space
A simple and distraction-free Study Tracker designed to help students build consistent study habits and stay productive during exam preparation.
This tool allows students to log their study sessions, monitor their progress, and understand how they spend their study time across different subjects.

🔗 Live Demo:
https://academic-space.lovable.app/study-tracker

🎯 Purpose
Many students struggle with:
Tracking how much they actually study
Managing time effectively
Maintaining consistency during exam periods
The Study Tracker solves this by providing a minimal interface where students can record study sessions and analyze their progress over time.

✨ Features
⏱ Study Session Logging
Record study sessions with subject and duration
Keep track of daily study hours
📊 Progress Tracking
View study activity over time
Identify which subjects receive the most focus

📅 Consistency Building

Encourage daily study habits
Monitor productivity patterns

🎓 Student-Friendly Design

Simple and clean interface
Quick and distraction-free usage

🧠 How It Helps Students

Using a study tracker can:
Improve time management
Increase study consistency
Help identify weak subjects
Provide motivation through visible progress
